Cold Anchor (TIP)

Cold water on the face or wrists, or an ice cube in the hand, triggers the dive reflex and slows heart rate within ~30 seconds. Hold 15–30s while breathing out slowly. (Check with your doctor first if you have a heart condition.)

Quick defusion moves

Fast Untangles

  • Label it: "I'm having the thought that ___." Then: "I notice I'm having the thought that ___." Watch the distance open up.
  • Thank your mind: "Thanks, brain. Very creative. Filed under fiction."
  • Silly voice: Repeat the scary thought in a cartoon villain voice. Dread rarely survives a bad accent.
  • Name the channel: "Ah — Radio Doom & Gloom, broadcasting again. Same three hits."
  • The word drill: Say the scariest word in the thought out loud 30 times fast until it dissolves into noise.
